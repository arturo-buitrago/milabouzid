**Costa Rican-born and Germany-based, Mila Bouzid is a vocalist known for her warm tone, viral scale improvisations, and powerful soprano agility. With a repertoire spanning pop, soul, jazz, boleros, and bossa, she has established herself as a versatile live performer across orchestral productions, television appearances and private events.** 

In Costa Rica, she performed regularly with the Philharmonic Orchestra, one of the region’s most prestigious ensembles. Since relocating to Germany, she has appeared on German television and performs regularly at private events across the country. With an established track record as a live vocalist, she is now preparing her debut studio album, projected for release in spring 2027.

Based in Germany, Mila Bouzid is available for bookings worldwide.
